**ReelForge AI v1.1.0** introduces creative color grading via custom 3D LUT import (`.cube`), professional animated caption styles and kinetic presets, intelligent multi-track audio mixing with speech auto-ducking, expanded transition options, and a completely streamlined consumer-focused Updates experience.

---

## v1.1.0 Release Highlights

* **Original-Pixel Editing**: Strictly non-generative. Preserves authentic camera footage, colors, and textures without synthesizing deepfakes, AI faces, or artificial hallucinations.
* **Custom 3D LUT Color Grading**: Import industry standard `.cube` LUT files directly into clips with 1-click "Apply LUT to all", seeded with 4 bundled cinematic LUTs (*Teal and Orange*, *Warm Golden*, *Moody Film Noir*, *Clean Vivid*).
* **Advanced Animated Captions**: 5 professional typography presets (*TikTok Bold Yellow*, *Minimal White*, *Cinematic Box*, *Neon Cyan*, *Classic*) and 4 kinetic animations (*Pop-in*, *Slide Up*, *Smooth Fade*, *Static*).
* **Multi-Track Audio Mixing & Ducking**: Independent Background Music (BGM) track and Voiceover track with smart speech auto-ducking powered by sidechain compression.
* **Expanded Transitions**: Smooth cinematic transitions including Cut, Fade, Crossfade, Wipe Left, Wipe Right, Slide Up, Slide Down, and Dissolve.
* **Reference Style Learning**: Automatically extracts scene cuts, shot duration cadence, and rhythm from viral short-form reels to guide your timeline edits.
* **Local Pacing ML Intelligence**: Trains an offline CPU logistic regression model on your footage and editing feedback to personalize cut timing.
* **Multi-Track Timeline**: Features beat-snapping, dialogue auto-captions, creative color presets, per-clip audio control (-24 dB to +12 dB), and 50-step undo/redo.
* **Simplified Folder Navigation**: Direct Windows File Explorer shortcuts, disk capacity meters, and native OS drag-and-drop.
* **Streamlined Consumer Updates & Rollback**: Clean in-app update checks, 1-click version rollback with zero git/repo clutter, and offline installer management.
